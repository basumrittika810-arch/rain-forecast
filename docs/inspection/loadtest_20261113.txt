Load test of the live Vaanam site, 13 Nov 2026 (D-034)
=======================================================
Site:     https://rain-forecast-api-qii4.onrender.com  (Render free plan: 0.1 CPU, 512 MB - documented)
Client:   the owner's Windows laptop, home internet; Python 3.12, httpx
Forecast: the 12 Nov 2026 00 UTC run was being served
Script:   scripts/loadtest.py (run 2 below used the same logic as the saved script)
One visit = 8 page files in parallel, 2 searches, 20 % misspelt search, 30 % GPS lookup, 1 forecast, 1-3 s pause.
Each step lasted 20 s. "p95" = 95 % of requests were at least this fast.

What this shows / does not show
-------------------------------
Shows (verified): 35 people using the site at the same moment, about 225 visits a minute (~13,000 an hour),
  zero errors; pages slow down gradually (whole-visit p95 5.7 s at 35) rather than breaking.
Does not show (open):
  - the breaking point: the test stopped at 35 users as planned, so the real ceiling is higher but unknown;
  - wake-up from sleep: after 15 min without visitors the free server sleeps and the first visit waits 30-60 s
    (documented by Render, not measured here);
  - slow or patchy Indian mobile networks (the test ran from one home connection).
